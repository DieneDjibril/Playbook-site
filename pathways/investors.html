Before you look at models

Questions worth asking first

These shape how you'll read the models and cases below — they don't point to one model on their own.

Who pays, and is that credible?

Is there a validated payer, purchaser, or offtaker — not just a beneficiary?

Can it repeat?

Is the model standardized enough to scale without being redesigned each time?
